# NIRO — IT JUST FLOWS · Director's treatment (V3)

**Message:** "Your desktop shouldn't get in your way. It should move with you."
**Length:** 45.000 s · 3840×2160 16:9 and 1080×1920 9:16 · 60 fps · music: the supplied track, excerpt 28.5–73.5 s.

## Why V2 failed, and what changes
V2 was a tour of floating panels in blue space. Nothing caused anything, the camera moved all the time, the subject changed every second and the music was only underneath. V3 is directed instead:

* **One world, grounded.** After the opening macros every shot is a real NIRO desktop on a real wallpaper. No floating windows, no thick glass sides; glass is shown the way it works: translucency over a wallpaper that has something in it.
* **Cause and effect.** The pointer does something, the desktop answers: pill → player → music; Files → file → editor; Super+Space → query → Control Panel; accent click → the whole desktop changes.
* **One subject per shot.** The viewer always knows where to look; the camera moves only when the interface is still (S05 is the one deliberate exception: a pull-back while the Island is the only thing happening).
* **Cuts are decisions.** Hard cuts on measured musical onsets; one match cut (dock click → opened window); one controlled pull-back. No portals, no glass morphs, no zoom-throughs, no particles.
* **Rest.** S03 (a held pause on the pill) and S11 (the finished desktop) are there to be looked at.

## Music
The track is analysed (spectral-flux onsets, band energies, 60 fps envelope); the excerpt is chosen so that the **quiet verse** carries S01–S03, the **chorus entry** lands on the Play press (9.81 s, the strongest onset of the phrase), the **dip** at 33 s carries the Control Panel before the change, and the **return of energy** at 35.6 s carries the accent click (the strongest onset of the film). See `sync-map.md`. Sound effects are sparse and small, so the song stays the lead. No voice-over.

## Authenticity rules
Everything on a screen is a real capture of NIRO (private GNOME session, black/white pair per element, true alpha recovered). Not shown: unsupported features, capture artefacts, a recoloured profile label, unrelated text in the editor. Composed (and said so in `qc-report.md`): the wallpaper (a designed one, so glass can be seen), the Island's visualiser (in the empty half of the player, driven by the real track), the logotype, the light wave of the accent change (the colours it reveals are real captures of the second accent), cursor, keycaps, "Make it yours.".
Rights: public use of the track needs sync and master-use licences; the film is for review until they are confirmed.
